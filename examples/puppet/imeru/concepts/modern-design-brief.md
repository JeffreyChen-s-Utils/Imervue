# Modern full-body Imeru

The full-body illustration is a design reference. The executable model and its
runtime previews are separate deliverables.

The design combines modern Japanese anime anatomy and facial drawing with
fashion tailoring, restrained science-fiction streetwear and Art Deco celestial
ornaments. Adult proportions are approximately seven heads tall. The entire
silhouette includes the shoulders, waist, arms, hips, legs and boots.

The outfit uses an asymmetric ivory short jacket over a charcoal high-neck top,
contrasting sleeves, fitted shorts with an angular side panel, dark opaque
leggings and structured boots. Small brass orbital clasps and a rust-orange tab
carry the celestial motif. The previous sailor collar and large neck bow are
removed.

## Illustration prompt

Create a production character design for Imeru, an original adult female anime
desktop companion, fully redesigned entire body. One full-body front view,
neutral relaxed A-pose with arms slightly away from torso, both hands visible
with natural fingers, feet fully visible, character alone on genuinely
transparent background. Modern premium Japanese anime illustration,
approximately 7 heads tall, small refined face, natural moderately broad
shoulders, slim athletic adult build, clearly defined waist, long elegantly
proportioned legs, NOT chibi, NOT childlike, NOT giant head. Cohesive fusion of
contemporary anime character art, fashion editorial tailoring, restrained sci-fi
techwear, and Art Deco celestial motifs. Dark midnight teal asymmetrical medium
hair with flowing locks and a single warm ivory streak, amber eyes, expressive
clean linework. Outfit entirely newly designed: short asymmetrical ivory
tailored jacket with fitted dark charcoal high-neck inner top, tasteful brass
orbital clasps, one dark teal sleeve and one ivory sleeve, minimal rust-orange
ribbon tab, high-waisted asymmetric charcoal shorts with a short angular side
overskirt, opaque fitted dark leggings, tailored mid-calf boots with small
ivory/brass accents. Refined clothed nonsexual character, no cleavage. Strong
readable silhouette and elegant garment shapes. Fine ink lines, crisp modern
anime cel shading with restrained soft reflected light, richer modern
illustration quality, no plastic 3D rendering. No star-covered sailor uniform,
no large round bow, no old-fashioned schoolgirl look, no text, no watermark,
no panels, no props, no scenery. Leave transparent margin around full body.
This image is a visual design reference for later layered Puppet construction.
